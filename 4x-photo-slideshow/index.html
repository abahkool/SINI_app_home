Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the desktop app and pricing.

What's the difference between the free extension and the $12 desktop app?

The Chrome extension has the full slideshow engine but is limited by the browser sandbox — it can't save native file paths persistently, can't detach the sidebar into a separate window, and has no video export. It also shows a small "4X Photo Slideshow" watermark. The desktop app removes all these limits and runs as a native Windows application.

Is it really a one-time payment? No subscription?

Yes — $12 one-time, lifetime access. No recurring charges. All bug-fix updates are included. Major version upgrades may be offered at a discounted rate.

How many computers can I install it on?

Up to 3 personal devices per license. For commercial or multi-seat deployments, contact us for volume pricing.

Do you collect my data or photos?

No. The app runs 100% offline. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no tracking. Your photos, audio files, folder paths, and settings never leave your computer. Payment data is handled by Polar.sh — we never see your credit card.

Can I run the desktop app on a single monitor?

Yes. Presenter Mode lets you detach the configuration panel into its own window and position it wherever you like — ideal for multi-monitor setups. On a single monitor you can keep it on top or toggle it with the keyboard (B / Ctrl+B).

Can I record the slideshow as a video?

Yes (desktop app only). Use the built-in Video Export feature which captures the entire slideshow area plus system audio. Choose your output format — WebM, MP4, AVI, or MKV. The app handles transcoding automatically via bundled FFmpeg. Ideal for creating timelapse recordings or sharing presentation videos.

What's the watermark situation in the free version?

The Chrome extension displays a small "4X Photo Slideshow" watermark centered in the slideshow area. The desktop app has no watermark — your presentation is completely clean.

Can I use this for commercial events?

Yes. The license covers personal and commercial use (events, digital signage, photo booths, etc.) on up to 3 devices per purchase. For larger deployments, contact us.

How do I buy? What payment methods are accepted?

Purchases are processed via Polar.sh, a secure payment platform. They accept credit/debit cards and PayPal. No account is required on our side — you receive a download link after purchase.
